Combined changelog for the MCRN Corvette page on NexusMods.

The page carries two files, each with its own version numbers: the ships and ModdedShipLoader.
NexusMods keeps one changelog per page and shows them all in one dropdown, so every ModdedShipLoader line starts
with its name. The ships are the page's main file, and their lines carry no prefix. One block per version: the first line is the version to enter, the lines
under it are the changelog lines.

TheExpanse has its own page; its changelog is in the ShipbreakerMods repository, TheExpanse/NexusMods_Changelog.txt.


=== ModdedShipLoader (our fork of PieMods v1.2.0) ===

1.2.2
ModdedShipLoader v1.2.2-kb1: fixed large custom ships closing the game during the loading screen. While joining a ship's parts, the game set aside memory in proportion to the part count times the size of the most detailed part: about 36 GB for a ship of 5,700 parts. That is now a few hundred MB, and the same ship loads with the game using about 8.5 GB in total.
ModdedShipLoader v1.2.2-kb1: fixed very large ships crashing on any PC, however much memory it has. The size limit that caused it is gone.
ModdedShipLoader v1.2.2-kb1: added a memory report in BepInEx\LogOutput.log, on lines starting with [MSL-Diag]: Windows version, CPU, RAM, graphics card, free drive space, and memory free and used before and after a ship's parts are joined. No user name, PC name or file paths are recorded.

1.2.1
ModdedShipLoader v1.2.1-kb1: fixed continuing an in-progress custom ship in Career after restarting the game, which broke the ship. Parts lost their scanner info and highlight and could not be grappled or cut.
ModdedShipLoader v1.2.1-kb1: fixed the Career job board showing the previous card's image and milestones for a custom ship read from a save, or for a ship type without rewards.
ModdedShipLoader v1.2.1-kb1: fixed hazards, core systems and mass on the job board being wrong on the first card of a session.
ModdedShipLoader v1.2.1-kb1: one missing asset no longer stops a whole ship from spawning.


=== Ships (MCRN Tachi, Rocinante, Beratnas Gas Hauler) ===

0.9.13
Fuel pipes: the red fuel pipes can now be cut at cutter level 1 and by demo charges, like the stock ones.
Backup power cell emergency lights always work; they no longer roll the random broken or flickering chance.
Galley: the lower kitchen cupboard joints the wall again.
Fewer invisible walls: the aft point defense nests, Engineering 25 and 27, the heat exchangers, two Engineering floor edges, both filters and the torpedo tube collars were split or closed with hull plating, so their solid shape now matches what you see. Pipes that sat inside them can now be reached, cut, scanned and blasted.
Torpedoes now weigh about 600 kg each instead of 10 kg, and still explode like a reactor when destroyed.
Hanging spacesuits are now suit patch kit pickups.
Gloves and boots in the airlocks no longer have an unfinished pickup prompt.
Airlock: the second helmet on Deck 9 can be picked up again.
Gas Hauler: the point defense cover doors have a darker, matte paint.

0.9.12
Generators: all four are now the stock generator with its three status lights, so each shows when its fuses are out and powers down safely once every breaker is pulled.
Backup power cells moved beside the dorsal and ventral torpedo tubes, where they are easier to find.
Engineering ceiling: the gaps where the hull walls meet the ceiling are closed.
Tunnel tiles no longer hold on to three hull parts at spawn.
Reactor: the eight plasma injector nozzles are now a single thruster nozzle each.
Reactor room: 16 reaction mass tanks hang under the cryo deck plates, between the injector arms.
Rocinante: the two railgun generators are now full stock generators like the other four, so pulling every breaker powers them down too.
Ammo posters: a point defense ammo poster on every stack of ammo crates.
Rocinante: a railgun slug poster on the railgun's loader crate, which holds 20 slugs. The crate now stands with its poster side to the room.
Rocinante: two generators on the railgun breech. They carry the shock hazard: pull the breakers before you take them off.
Point defense cannons: each gun's rotor is now part of its truss, with a thin plate on the gun, so the guns no longer come loose at spawn on some builds (6 parts fewer).
Hanging spacesuits use a lighter mesh, which raises the ship's part ceiling.
Weapons Storage: the spare point defense rotor is replaced by a spare targeting eye.

0.9.11
Hull paint on the MCRN Tachi and Gas Hauler matched to the Rocinante: sharper paint, hull plating visible, less metallic.
Engineering hull: inside plates that showed from outside removed.
Cryo ring rebuilt with longer pipes; two cut points added on the ventral side.
Ghost ships: a 60th Protomolecule node, a large crystal on the reactor housing.

0.9.10
All six point defense cannon housings rebuilt from the original model: frames, collar, tunnel with recessed glowing coils, end plate and one truss.
Point defense rings cut in eight pieces with chamfered corners.
Gas Hauler: all six cannon nests are closed by two-leaf doors.
Fore hull regrouped with new cut points.
Fixed an invisible wall in front of the fore dorsal airlock, and an airlock tunnel on the command deck that would not release.
Mid cannon ammunition belts are continuous.

0.9.9
Rocinante has a keel railgun.
Each point defense cannon has a second rotor disc in its nest, and its coils glow.
Most data drives replaced by useful pickups: credit drives, oxygen and thruster fuel.
Cannon barrels and rotor discs show their names.
Fixed breach warnings around both airlocks on some spawns.

0.9.8
Ghost ships have far more to find and deal with, on every deck and outside.
Ghost ships look the part, inside and out.
The body aboard is no longer always in the same place, or always the same person.
Deck carpets are colored by department.
Hull plating shows surface relief.
Rocinante hull pattern reworked, new ship pictures.
MCRN Tachi has MCRN posters. The Rocinante and the Gas Hauler are fitted out for a smaller crew.
Aft cannons' ammunition feeds completed.
Torpedoes are more fragile.
Fixes to comms array recesses, a ceiling arm through a stair railing, and deck plating.
